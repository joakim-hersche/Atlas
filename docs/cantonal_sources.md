National / intercantonal hubs:
- geodienste.ch: Gefahrenkarten download host. Bulk files at geodienste.ch/downloads/gefahrenkarten (data_format=gpkg&cantons=XX); WFS at geodienste.ch/db/gefahrenkarten_v1_3_0/deu (also /db/gefahrenkarten/deu|fra).
- opendata.swiss, ckan.opendata.swiss: CKAN mirror of most cantonal OGD datasets.
- amstat.ch, www.amstat.ch: SECO AMSTAT; a statistik.bs.ch fact sheet says registered unemployment data exists down to Gemeinde level; the interactive DB may not be open (unverified).
- www.unil.ch: IDHEAP comparatif finances cantonales/communales (www.unil.ch/idheap/comparatif); www.badac.ch (inferred host for badac.ch).
- atlas.bfs.admin.ch, dam-api.bfs.admin.ch, www.secoalv.admin.ch: federal atlas and file hosts.
- No intercantonal HRM2 Gemeinde portal was found; HRM2 key ratios are published canton by canton.
