## Per-topic comparability and recommended v1 metric

### 1. Municipal finances (`kt_gemeindefinanzen_compiled`)
What exists:
- Ready-made HRM2 ratio tables: ZH (Gemeindeporträt CSV, 1990–2024), SO (GEFIN CSV export, 2021–2025), AG (eDossier T12, one year per edition), TG (sk-stat-4, 2019–2024), FR (18_02_indicateurs, 2022–), GR (data.gr.ch, 2019–), VD (Indicateurs par commune 2017–2025), GE (net debt per resident by commune, 2000–2024). SG STADA2 also has ratios (RMSG 2017–2025), but its host is blocked.
- Full accounts, so ratios must be computed: LU (gefis-lu-jr.csv, 2019–2025) and BL (10640, 2015–2025).
- Clean PDFs: NE (one table a year), SH, BE, TI.

Comparability:
- The HRM2 ratios have common definitions (FDK/KKAG recommendations): Nettoschuld I/EW = Fremdkapital − Finanzvermögen; SFG = Selbstfinanzierung / Nettoinvestitionen; Zinsbelastungsanteil = Nettozinsaufwand / laufender Ertrag. So a common metric can be defined.
- Caveats:
  - (a) Series break at the HRM1 → HRM2 switch, which happened in different years: BL 2014, ZH 2019, GE 2018, FR 2021/22, TI about 2022. VD still mixes MCH1 and MCH2 communes.
  - (b) Scope differs. ZH, TG and SG have separate school municipalities, so the political Gemeinde excludes school debt and spending, unlike Einheitsgemeinden elsewhere. Zweckverbände distort the figures too (SO warns about this).
  - (c) Signs differ: ZH publishes Nettovermögen (positive = assets), the others Nettoschuld.
  - (d) The SFG is sometimes a 5-year mean (SG, AG map) and sometimes annual.
- Operating expenditure per resident is not published as such anywhere. It can be computed only from full accounts (LU, BL, TG, FR 18_04) or approximated from net expenditure by function (ZH, AG T5). Tax revenue per resident is available for ZH, GE, AG, LU, BL and FR (needs a population denominator).

Recommended v1 metrics:
- **Net debt per resident (CHF, Nettoschuld I, HRM2, latest common year 2024):** 7 cantons verified as data (ZH sign-flipped, SO, AG, TG, GE, plus LU and BL computed). 9 if data.zg.ch and stada2.sg.ch are allowed. Up to 11 if the FR and GR indicator lists contain it, which is not yet verified. Up to 14 with the NE, SH and BE PDFs.
- **Self-financing ratio (%):** 9 cantons (ZH, FR, SO, AG, TG, VD, plus LU and BL computed, GR if listed). 10 with SG.
- Interest burden share as a third metric: same set as the self-financing ratio.
- Leave operating expenditure per resident out of v1.

### 2. Land / property prices (`kt_zh_bodenpreise_gemeinde`, `kt_other_transaction_prices`)
What exists:
- ZH: model median and transaction median for unbuilt building land, CHF/m², 1974–2024, plus sales counts.
- BL: mean transaction price of residential building land, CHF/m², 1979–2025, with number of cases.
- GE: house prices (quartiles, kCHF) and price per m² of condominium flats, by commune, 1990–2024.
- TG: tax-valuation min/max per zone. This is not a market price.

Comparability:
- Only ZH and BL measure the same thing (residential building land, CHF/m²). They still differ in statistic (median/model vs mean) and in small-number handling: ZH suppresses values with fewer than 4 cases, BL publishes everything.
- GE measures a different price type (dwellings).

Recommended v1:
- **"Residential building land price, CHF/m² (transactions)": 2 cantons (ZH, BL)**, with GE as a separate "house price median" metric (1 canton).
- Coverage is too thin for a national-looking map. Keep the D5 regional fallback as the main layer and show these as `*` local detail.

### 3. Police crime statistics (`kt_crime_gemeinde_compiled`)
What exists:
- The cantonal PKS annual reports are produced on a **BFS template**. Each has table 4.1.3 "Strafgesetzbuch: Straftaten nach Gemeinde" for all Gemeinden over 7 years and 2.3.1.4 "Häufigkeitszahlen nach Gemeinde", with the footer "Quelle: BFS – PKS".
- Verified for LU, SO, FR, GR, SZ and GE (in French). BE publishes its own clean all-Gemeinden extract with Einwohner and Häufigkeitszahl. VD has one PDF per district with one page per commune.
- Machine-readable: ZH (OGD CSV, Gemeinden over 10k plus Stadtkreise, 2009–2025) and BS (data.bs.ch, 3 Gemeinden).

Comparability: high. Every canton uses the same PKS counting rules, so the definitions match.

Recommended v1:
- **StGB offences per 1,000 residents (Häufigkeitszahl), latest year 2025**:
  - 10 cantons (ZH partial, BS, LU, SO, FR, GR, SZ, GE, BE, VD) with PDF parsing.
  - Up to about 15 with the unverified NE, AR, AI, GL and BL reports.
- **Best route:** since the numbers are BFS PKS output, ask BFS for the PKS Gemeinde table (all cantons, 2009–) instead of parsing about 15 PDFs. No public BFS asset was found (DAM extendedSearch "Straftaten nach Gemeinde" returned none).
- Small Gemeinden give noisy rates. Use multi-year sums, or show values only above a population threshold.

### 4. Pupils / schools (`kt_pupils_schools_compiled`)
What exists:
- ZH: pupils by level per politische Gemeinde, 1990–2023.
- BL: 10420, learners by Wohngemeinde, 2014–.
- FR: pupils at residence and at school location, and classes, 2010–2024.
- TG and SG: by school district (needs a crosswalk).
- LU: Gemeindeprofile PDFs only.

Comparability:
- BL and FR state that the source is the **BFS Statistik der Lernenden (SDL)** counted at the residence commune. So a national definition exists, and BFS holds it at Gemeinde level.
- TG and SG school districts don't map 1:1 to Gemeinden.

Recommended v1:
- **Pupils in compulsory school (primary + lower secondary) by residence Gemeinde, per 1,000 residents:** 3 cantons (ZH, BL, FR). 5 if school districts are allowed via a crosswalk.
- Because the source is BFS SDL, a BFS data request would give all 26. That beats a cantonal compilation.

### 5. Registered unemployment (`kt_unemployment_gemeinde_compiled`)
What exists:
- ZH: counts 1991–2024 and share of the population aged 15–64.
- GE: annual means 1990–2025.
- VD: latest month only (about 150 communes).
- AG: monthly PDFs, Gemeinden with fewer than 5 unemployed suppressed.
- SG: STADA2, 2009–2026, blocked.
- BS: Riehen only.
- FR and TG OGD are canton-level only.

Comparability:
- All of these are SECO AVAM extracts, so the definition is the same (registered unemployed).
- Denominators differ: ZH uses the population aged 15–64, VD and SG use the SECO labour force, GE publishes counts only.
- Suppression thresholds differ too (fewer than 5 in ZH and AG).

Recommended v1:
- **Registered unemployed (annual mean) per 100 residents aged 15–64, computed by Gemeindebank from counts plus STATPOP:**
  - 2 cantons with history (ZH, GE).
  - 4 with SG (host needed) and AG (12 PDFs a year).
  - VD only as a current snapshot.
- Because every source is SECO, the national route (the SECO amstat Gemeinde table, see `seco_amstat_unemployment`) is the better investment.

### 6. Hazard maps (`cantonal_gefahrenkarten`)
What exists:
- geodienste.ch lists all 26 cantons under one data model, MGDM 166.1 v1.3.
- **21 cantons are freely downloadable** as GPKG, SHP or INTERLIS, and through WFS and OGC API: AG, AI, AR, BE, BL, FR, GE, GL, GR, JU, LU, NE, SG, SH, SO, SZ, TG, TI, VS, ZG, ZH.
- 4 cantons need approval ("Freigabe erforderlich"; the download returns HTTP 401): NW, OW, UR, VD. They were not requested, per the brief.
- BS is "Im Aufbau" (404).
- Licence terms vary:
  - Most: "Freie Nutzung. Quellenangabe ist Pflicht".
  - AR, GL, SH, SO, ZH: "Freie Nutzung".
  - NE and UR: commercial use only with permission.
  - GR and SG: "keine Angabe" (own terms pages).

Comparability: good, because of the common model. Key layer: `synoptisches_gefahrengebiet` with `maximale_gefahrenstufe` (nicht_gefaehrdet / gering / mittel / erheblich / Restgefährdung) and by process (wasser / rutschung / sturz / lawine).
- Caveat: maps cover only the surveyed perimeters (`erhebungsgebiet`), usually the settlement area. "Not assessed" must be shown as such (D21), not as "no hazard".
- Update dates range from 2022 to 2026.

Recommended v1:
- **Share of the Gemeinde's building-zone (or GWR building) footprint in the high (erheblich, red) or medium (mittel, blue) hazard level: 21 cantons. 25 if the 4 approvals are obtained.**
- The ZH INTERLIS zip is 724 MB. Plan GPKG per canton, or use OGC API.
